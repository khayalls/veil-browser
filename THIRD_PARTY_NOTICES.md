## CEF and Chromium

DEPENDENCIES.json identifies the official CEF macOS arm64 archive and its hash. Binaries are downloaded locally, not committed. Builds include the distribution's LICENSE.txt as CEF-LICENSE.txt and CREDITS.html as Chromium-CREDITS.html. Preserve applicable notices when distributing builds.

## Apple frameworks and original material

Apple frameworks, fonts and SF Symbols are accessed through system APIs, not bundled as copied font/symbol libraries. The VEIL icon is generated by scripts/make-icon.swift. No Arc logo, private Figma file or personal-site screenshot is included.

No license has been selected for VEIL's original code/assets. Third-party licenses continue to govern their own material.
